SCP-3997

Object Class: Safe

Entry Type: Symbiote

Special Containment Procedures

  • Physical Requirements: A separate cage/electricity resistance double-sided/two-way panel should be installed inside the durable metal cages. In the event that the telephone rings, please use metal tweezers. If the space filler's burrow is deep into the organic solid body, please immediately use the panhandle, milk them down, and put them into the electric cage.
  • Security Measures: Authorized personnel are not allowed to come less than 100 meters from the spaces, and extra caution should be taken to cover large distances. Please utilize gas body scanners if there is any doubt about the body's absence in order to protect the/Lady/Supt Sheriff from possible danger.
  • Handling Instructions: The main focus should be on keeping SCP-3990's unique body physical health intact. All personnel must either drive a bicycle, or use given facilities in order to reach the designated storage warehouse.

Description

Physical Appearance: A parasite that attaches itself to a vehicle, an object, or a person, according to its host's preference. The symbiote does not sound harmful in general, but it is impossible to guess how it can treat humans,

Anomalous Properties: The worst thing about the SCP-3997 is that its main feature is the ability to adapt to the host's appearance in order to provide a better chance of survival through camouflage. However, this is only a secondary concern. SCP-3997 is able to maintain all the abilities of its host when attached to it, and does not lose any of its powers upon the host's death.
