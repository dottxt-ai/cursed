SCP-1232

Object Class: Safe

Entry Type: Spatial Anomaly

Special Containment Procedures

  • Physical Requirements:
  • Security Measures: Access to SCP-1232 is restricted to Foundation personnel only. The object is housed in a secure location, and all access to it is monitored. Any unauthorized attempts to access SCP-1232 are to be immediately reported and investigated.
  • Handling Instructions: Personnel handling SCP-1232 must wear protective clothing and gear at all times. Gloves and goggles are required, as the surface of SCP-1232 can cause burns and irritation.

Description

Physical Appearance: The anomaly appears to be a sphere with a drill protruding from its surface. The sphere itself is made of a metallic substance, while the drill is composed of a black, oily substance. The diameter of the sphere is approximately 2 meters, and the drill protrudes from the surface by approximately 1 meter.

Anomalous Properties: When an object is inserted into SCP-1232, the object will be consumed by the cage and will disappear. After a few seconds, the object will reappear outside of the cage, as if it had been teleported. This process can be repeated as many times as desired.

Origin: The exact origin of SCP-1232 is unknown. It was found in a remote location in the desert, and its purpose is still unknown.

Relevant History: SCP-1232 was first discovered by a group of hikers who found it in the desert. They contacted the SCP Foundation and requested that it be contained, which was done quickly and without incident.
